# Third-party materials

[English](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) | [简体中文](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.zh-CN.md)

This is a navigation aid to the bundled materials, not a replacement for their licenses or a completed redistribution audit. Check the original package terms and obtain required licenses before reusing or distributing any material.
