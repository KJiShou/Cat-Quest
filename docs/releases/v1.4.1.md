# Cat Quest v1.4.1 — Equipment Sparkles and Boss Art

## What's changed

- Legendary and Demon equipment now share bright golden stars and floating lights across loot results, inventory, equipped slots, Dashboard, ranking profiles, equipment details and hover previews. Demon mastery reward cards also sparkle before and after collection.
- Loot-result effects continue until the results close. Effects stop when their view closes and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Refreshed the nine Boss equipment illustrations to match the loot equipment art style.
- Added a clearer equipped icon to inventory items.
- Moved Focus Insights date navigation above the graph, raised the tag section and repositioned Today.

Equipment stats, rewards, ownership and save rules are unchanged. This release updates the Windows application; the hosted Web demo is unchanged.

## Verification

- Isolated Unity Play Mode checks passed for loot results, inventory, equipped slots, detail/hover previews, ranking equipment and Demon mastery claims, including effect cleanup and claim persistence.
- Reviewed affected equipment screens at 1920×1080 and 1280×720.
- Built the non-development Windows x64 player with zero build errors. The actual v1.4.0 updater installed the local v1.4.1 package, restarted the new player and retained the isolated SQLite fixture; both players accepted normal window-close requests.
- The older command-driven release smoke script is incompatible with production builds because its polling is Development-only. This release used native process/update checks instead; production Pet Widget interaction and the full online in-app download flow were not re-verified.
